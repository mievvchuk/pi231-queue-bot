# Lab Count Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** Store planned lab count per queue entry and display an approximate duration.

**Architecture:** Nullable lab_count in both adapters; queue services validate and persist it. A focused Telegram lab-count flow shares the existing student router and only creates a join after the response. Shared entry formatting shows the estimate.

**Tech Stack:** Python, aiogram, SQLite/PostgreSQL, pytest.

**Spec:** ../specs/2026-10-26-lab-count-design.md

## Global Constraints
- Counts 1–20 or NULL, baseline 7–10 minutes, 2 labs ≈15–20 minutes.
- Preserve ordering, repeatable swaps, historical data; no update broadcasts.
- Legacy entries have no estimate until edited. No live DB or deploy changes.

## Review Focus
- Abandoned signup must not create an entry or reserve a position.
- Late closed/archive/ban changes must be enforced at final submission.
- Leaving and filling a vacant slot must not inherit the prior count.
- Quantity must follow the student on swap/transfer, not the position.
- Old SQLite/PG schemas must upgrade without replacing users or queue entries.
